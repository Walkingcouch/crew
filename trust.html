How payments work

Money moves only when the job is done.

Crew's escrow model means neither party takes on payment risk. Customers know their money won't disappear. Contractors know they'll get paid — no chasing invoices.

1

Customer pre-authorises payment

When a job is booked, funds are authorised and held in a secure escrow account. The contractor can see the funds are ready before they show up.

2

Contractor completes the job

Work is completed and marked done in the app. Photos, notes, and job details are logged automatically as a record for both parties.

3

Payment is released automatically

Once the job is confirmed complete, funds are released within 24 hours. No invoicing required. No follow-up calls needed.

4

Dispute? We step in.

If there's ever a disagreement, our team reviews the job record and mediates fairly. Payment is held until resolved.

Data security

Your data is protected at every layer.

Crew is built on infrastructure with bank-grade security. We don't cut corners — your personal and financial data is encrypted, isolated, and access-controlled.

TLS 1.3 encryption in transit

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Man-in-the-middle attacks are prevented by design.

AES-256 encryption at rest

All data stored in our databases is encrypted at rest using AES-256. Even if the underlying storage were compromised, your data would remain unreadable.

Role-based access control

Crew employees follow strict least-privilege access. No engineer can access customer payment data without multi-party approval and a logged audit trail.

Hosted in Australia

All primary data is stored in AWS ap-southeast-2 (Sydney). Your data never leaves Australia without your knowledge and consent.

Multi-factor authentication

MFA is available for all accounts and required for admin roles. We support authenticator apps and hardware security keys.

Continuous security monitoring

Automated threat detection runs 24/7. Unusual access patterns trigger alerts. Our security team responds to incidents within 1 hour.

Australian compliance

Built for Australian law, not retrofitted to it.

We designed Crew for the Australian market from day one. Our legal and compliance obligations are built into the product — not bolted on.

Crew complies fully with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and all 13 Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). You have the right to access, correct, and request deletion of your personal information at any time.
