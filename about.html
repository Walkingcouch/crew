Crew started as a simple question: why is it still so hard to find a trustworthy tradie? We set out to fix that, for homeowners and for the tradespeople who deserve better tools to run their businesses.

A platform built on fairness, for both sides

In 2023, we were helping a friend find a reliable lawn mowing service after two no-shows and a cash job gone wrong. The existing platforms either charged customers through the roof or took so much commission that good contractors couldn't afford to use them.

We believed a marketplace could be different: transparent pricing, secure escrow so customers aren't left out of pocket, and fair commission rates that let contractors actually build a business.

Crew launched in beta in early 2024 across Melbourne. By mid-2025 we were operating nationally, adding CrewBase for larger operations managing dozens of crews.

What we stand for

Three principles that shape every product decision we make

Trust above all

Escrow protects customers. Verified profiles protect contractors. Transparent pricing protects everyone. We won't ship a feature that compromises any of the three.

Fair to both sides

A marketplace dies if one side extracts too much from the other. We keep commission rates honest, enforce no surprises in billing, and give contractors tools that respect their time.

Built for Australia

GST-compliant invoices, Australian Privacy Act compliance, AEST-based support, and a product built with Australian trades and property types in mind, not adapted from overseas.
