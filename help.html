Payments & Escrow
When you approve a quote, the total is held in a secure escrow account � your money never goes directly to the contractor until the job is complete. Once you confirm the work is done (or after a 48-hour auto-release window), the funds are released. If there's a dispute, our team mediates before any release occurs.
Payment is authorised when work begins � not when you approve the quote. You won't be charged until the contractor checks in at your property via the app. If the job is cancelled before start, no charge is applied.
If you're unhappy with the work, tap Report an issue inside the job detail screen within 48 hours of completion. Our support team reviews all disputes within one business day. Funds in escrow cannot be released to the contractor while a dispute is open.
Yes. Every completed job automatically generates a GST-compliant tax invoice in your account under Invoices & Receipts. You can download as PDF or email directly from the portal.
Bookings & Scheduling
Submit a service request with your address, service type and preferred dates. Matched contractors in your area send you quotes within 24 hours. Review, compare and approve the quote that suits you � no payment until work begins.
You can reschedule or cancel at no cost up to 24 hours before the job start time. Cancellations within 24 hours may incur a call-out fee at the contractor's discretion, which is disclosed in their quote terms. Tap Reschedule from the job card in your portal.
Yes � after a completed job you can mark a contractor as a preferred worker. Future requests at the same property will be prioritised to them. You can manage preferred workers in your Properties settings.
Account & Profile
Go to Profile ? Account Settings in your portal or app. Email changes require re-verification via OTP. Phone number changes also trigger a verification SMS. For security, you cannot change both at the same time.
You can request account deletion from Profile ? Account Settings ? Delete Account. Per the Australian Privacy Act 1988, we retain transaction records for 7 years for tax compliance, but all personal data is anonymised within 30 days of deletion. Active bookings must be completed or cancelled first.
For Contractors
Sign up at getcrew.com.au/signup and select Join as contractor. You'll need to provide your ABN, proof of insurance, and any trade licences relevant to your services. Verification typically takes 1�2 business days.
Escrow funds are released to your nominated bank account within 2 business days of the customer confirming job completion (or automatic release after 48 hours if no response). No minimum payout threshold � every job pays out individually.
Crew charges a commission on completed jobs � never on quotes or cancellations. Rates start at 12% and reduce to 8% for high-volume contractors. See our full commission schedule for details.
