Crew Stamp Card

Book 5, get your
6th on us.

Every completed booking with a new contractor stamps your card, or once per calendar month per contractor for recurring bookings. Hit 5 and your next booking is commission-free. It resets and you start again.

Gold & Platinum members: every booking counts as 2 stamps, so you complete your card in 3 bookings instead of 5.
Commission waiver, not a discount
Your contractor is paid in full as normal. Crew waives its commission on that booking, you pay only the job price.
Resets automatically
After your free booking, the card resets and you immediately start earning toward the next one.
Stamps expire after 6 months inactive
Any booking resets the clock. You'll receive a reminder at 5 months if your stamps are at risk.
Recurring bookings, one stamp per contractor per month
If you have a regular contractor (e.g. weekly mowing), they contribute one stamp to your card per calendar month regardless of how many times they visit. Each new contractor you book earns a separate stamp.

FAQ

Common questions.

Can't find what you're looking for? Email us at hello@getcrew.com.au

Every completed booking earns you 100 CrewPoints per $1 spent. Points are added automatically to your account once the job is confirmed complete, no scanning, no codes required. You can also earn bonus points through the Rising Star boost (2× points when booking a new contractor), weekly streaks, and your Crew Stamp Card (book 5, get the 6th commission-free).
Points expiry is tiered based on your loyalty status. Bronze and Silver members: points expire after 3 months without a booking. Gold members: 6 months. Platinum members: 9 months. CrewPass subscribers: points never expire while your subscription is active, this is one of the core benefits of the paid subscription. Any booking resets the clock regardless of tier. We'll send a reminder at the halfway point of your expiry window so you have time to book or redeem. Crew Bucks (earned from milestones) never expire.
Points spend on add-ons listed by your contractor, things like green waste removal, weed spray, lawn fertiliser and more. Your contractor sets their own add-on prices; Crew pays them their listed rate when you redeem. Points are not redeemable for cash or as a discount off the base job price. Not all contractors offer add-ons, available options depend on your contractor's menu. Maximum $20 in add-on value per booking.
Loyalty discounts (5% Silver / 8% Gold / 10% Platinum) apply to the base service price only, not to add-ons, licensed trade jobs, or equipment hire. Each tier has a per-booking cap ($10 / $15 / $20 respectively). Discounts are applied automatically at checkout once you reach the qualifying tier. Tier status is calculated on your rolling 12-month spend.
Crew Bucks are real dollar credits earned from completing specific milestones, 4-week streaks, contractor referrals, and tier milestone bonuses. Unlike points (which spend on contractor add-ons), Crew Bucks apply directly to your next booking as a cash equivalent. They're applied automatically at checkout with no code needed, up to a maximum of $20 per booking. Crew Bucks never expire.
Every completed booking earns one stamp, with one important rule: if you have a recurring contractor (e.g. weekly mowing), that contractor contributes a maximum of one stamp per calendar month regardless of booking frequency. Collect 5 stamps and your 6th booking is commission-free: Crew waives our platform fee so you pay only your contractor, who is still paid in full. The one-stamp-per-contractor-per-month rule still applies for recurring services. Stamps expire after 6 months of account inactivity, and we'll remind you at 5 months. The free booking itself earns no points (since no commission changes hands), but resets your card immediately.
There's no cap on CrewPoints accumulation. You can earn as many as you like and take your time redeeming. Crew Bucks have a maximum $20 apply-per-booking limit, but there's no cap on your total Crew Bucks balance.
