02

Programme overview

The CrewPoints Rewards Programme ("the Programme") is operated by Crew Pty Ltd and is available to all registered Members. Participation constitutes acceptance of these Terms & Conditions in full.

The Programme consists of four distinct mechanics: CrewPoints (earn and spend on add-ons), Crew Bucks (milestone cash credits), the Crew Stamp Card (booking-frequency reward), and Loyalty Tiers (spend-based status benefits). Each mechanic operates independently.

Crew reserves the right to modify, suspend or terminate the Programme at any time with 30 days notice to Members, except where required by Australian Consumer Law.

05

Redemption — contractor add-ons

Points may only be redeemed for contractor add-on services. Points are not redeemable for cash, as a discount off the base job price, against booking fees, against licensed trade job costs, or for any service not listed as an add-on by the assigned Contractor.

💡
How payment works at redemption: When a Member redeems points for an add-on, Crew pays the Contractor their listed add-on rate directly from the Member's points balance — the Member pays nothing additional for that add-on. The Contractor is always paid in full at their listed rate.

Add-on pricing: Each Contractor sets their own add-on prices independently. Prices are not fixed by Crew and may vary between Contractors. Crew does not guarantee any specific add-on will be available or priced at any particular amount.

Add-on availability: Contractors may choose to offer add-ons, offer a selection of add-ons, or offer no add-ons at all. A Contractor's add-on menu is displayed at the time of booking. Crew makes no guarantee that any add-on will be available from any particular Contractor.

Per-booking cap: The maximum points redemption value per booking is $20.00. Points with a value exceeding $20 cannot be applied to a single booking. Unused points remain in the Member's balance.

Redemption rate: 100 CrewPoints = $0.10 in add-on value (10¢ per $1 spent earning). This rate may be changed by Crew with 30 days notice.

07

Crew Stamp Card

The Crew Stamp Card awards a commission-free booking after a Member accumulates a qualifying number of stamps. Each completed booking earns one stamp, subject to the recurring contractor rule below.

All members
Stamps required · reward
All members
5 bookings → 6th booking commission-free
🔄
Recurring bookings — one stamp per contractor per calendar month. Where a Member has an ongoing recurring booking arrangement with the same Contractor (weekly, fortnightly, or any regular frequency), that Contractor may contribute a maximum of one stamp to the Member's Stamp Card per calendar month, regardless of how many bookings are completed in that month. Each distinct Contractor booked in a given month earns a separate stamp. This rule applies equally to all members.

"Commission-free" defined: Crew waives its platform commission on the qualifying booking. The Contractor is paid in full at their normal rate. The Member pays only the job price — no platform fee is deducted. The booking fee ($2.99 for Basic members) is also waived on the qualifying booking.

Stamp card expiry: Stamps expire after 6 months of inactivity (no completed bookings). Any completed booking resets the stamp expiry clock. Expired stamps are forfeited and the card resets to zero. Crew will send a reminder at 5 months.

Stamp counting — recurring services: Each unique Contractor the Member books in a calendar month contributes one stamp, regardless of booking frequency with that Contractor. A Member booking the same lawn mowing contractor weekly earns one stamp in January, one in February, and so on. A Member who books three different contractors in one month earns three stamps in that month.

No points on free booking: The commission-free booking earns no CrewPoints, as no base service commission is collected. The stamp card resets immediately after the free booking is completed.

08

Contractor referral programme

🌿
Referring a contractor earns $15.00 in Crew Bucks, subject to the conditions below. This is the complete and binding statement of the contractor referral benefit.

8.1 — Eligibility and trigger

A Member earns $15.00 in Crew Bucks when all of the following conditions are satisfied:

  1. The Member shares their unique contractor referral code with a person who is not already registered as a Crew Contractor.
  2. That person registers as a Crew Contractor using the referral code within 30 days of the code being shared.
  3. The referred Contractor completes 3 separate, verified, paid jobs on the Crew platform. Each job must be accepted by a different customer, confirmed complete, and have payment released from escrow.

The $15.00 Crew Bucks credit is applied to the referring Member's account upon completion of the referred Contractor's 3rd qualifying job. No credit is awarded before this threshold is reached.

8.2 — Non-refundable

🔒
The $15.00 Crew Bucks credit is non-refundable and non-reversible once awarded. If the referred Contractor subsequently ceases operating on the Crew platform, is deactivated, or requests account deletion after the 3-job threshold has been met and the credit awarded, no clawback or reversal of the credit will occur. The credit remains in the referring Member's Crew Bucks balance.

8.3 — Limits and restrictions

The $15.00 credit is issued as Crew Bucks (see Section 6) and is subject to the Crew Bucks terms including the $20 per-booking cap and non-cash-redeemable nature. The credit has no monetary value outside the Crew platform.

10

CrewPass subscription

CrewPass is a paid monthly subscription available at $7.99/month. Benefits include: 1.25× CrewPoints multiplier, booking fee waiver, and points that never expire while the subscription is active and payments are current.

If a CrewPass subscription lapses, is cancelled, or payment fails: the booking fee waiver and multiplier cease immediately. The points never-expire benefit ceases from the lapse date, and the Member's tier-based expiry window applies from that date.

CrewPass does not affect Crew Bucks — Crew Bucks never expire regardless of subscription status.

Abuse, fraud and termination

Crew may suspend or terminate a Member's Programme participation, void accumulated points and Crew Bucks, and close the Member's account in the event of: suspected fraud, gaming of Programme mechanics, creation of fake accounts, coordinated referral abuse, or any breach of Crew's Terms of Use.

Points and Crew Bucks have no monetary value and Crew has no obligation to compensate a Member for forfeited points or credits in the event of account termination for cause.

⚠️
Crew uses automated fraud detection. Accounts flagged for abnormal earning patterns — including unusually high referral volumes, repeated same-contractor bookings, and booking cancellation-rebook patterns — will be reviewed and may be suspended pending investigation.
