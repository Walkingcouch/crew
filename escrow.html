Escrow Payments

Your money is held securely until the job is done and you're satisfied. No risk, no surprises.

1. What Is Escrow?

Escrow is a neutral payment arrangement where funds are held by Crew on behalf of both the customer and the contractor until a defined condition is met — in this case, your approval that the job has been completed satisfactorily.

Rather than paying a contractor directly before the work is done, your payment is deposited into a secure holding account. The contractor is guaranteed to receive payment once the job is approved; you are guaranteed not to lose money if the work is not delivered as agreed.

Key benefit: Neither party can walk away empty-handed. Customers are protected against non-delivery; contractors are protected against non-payment.

2. How It Works — Step by Step

1

Book & Pay

When you accept a quote, you enter your payment details and authorise the job amount. Funds are captured and held in a secure escrow account — they do not go to the contractor yet.

2

Job Completed

The contractor completes the work and marks the job as done in the Crew app. You receive a notification with photos and a completion summary where applicable.

3

You Approve

Review the work and tap Approve & Release in the app. You have up to 48 hours to review before funds are automatically released. If there's an issue, raise a dispute instead.

4

Contractor Paid

Funds are released from escrow to the contractor's registered bank account within 1–2 business days of approval. Crew's platform fee is deducted at this point.

3. When Funds Are Released

Funds held in escrow are released to the contractor under the following conditions:

  • You explicitly approve the job in the Crew app or Customer Portal.
  • 48 hours pass after the contractor marks the job complete and no dispute has been raised.
  • A dispute is resolved in the contractor's favour by the Crew Resolution Team.
Note: If you have not responded within 48 hours of a job being marked complete, funds are automatically released. Please review completed jobs promptly.

4. Disputes & Refunds

Raising a Dispute

If you are not satisfied with the work, tap Raise a Dispute in the app within 48 hours of the job being marked complete. Funds remain frozen in escrow while the dispute is active.

Resolution Process

The Crew Resolution Team will review evidence from both parties — photos, messages, job notes — and issue a binding decision within 5 business days. Possible outcomes include:

  • Full refund — if the work was not performed or was materially deficient.
  • Partial refund — where work was partially completed to an acceptable standard.
  • Full release to contractor — if the dispute is assessed as unsubstantiated.

Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.

Australian Consumer Law: Nothing in this policy limits or excludes rights you may have under the Australian Consumer Law (Schedule 2 of the Competition and Consumer Act 2010).

5. Fees & Timelines

Escrow Holding Fee

There is no separate escrow fee for customers. The escrow service is included as part of Crew's platform. Crew's commission is deducted from the contractor's payment at release.

Payment Methods

Crew accepts Visa, Mastercard, and Australian bank transfers (BPAY). All card payments are processed via Stripe with PCI DSS Level 1 compliance.

Holding Period

Funds are held in escrow from the point of booking until either approval, auto-release (48 hours post-completion), or dispute resolution. In the case of a cancelled job before any work begins, funds are returned to you within 3–5 business days.

6. Information for Contractors

As a Crew contractor, escrow protects you from non-payment. Once a customer books and pays, the funds are captured — you are guaranteed to receive payment if you complete the job as agreed.

  • Mark jobs complete in the Crew app as soon as work is finished.
  • Upload before/after photos where applicable — this strengthens your position if a dispute is raised.
  • Ensure your bank account details in Settings ? Payouts are current. Incorrect details will delay disbursement.
  • Payouts are processed within 1–2 business days of customer approval or auto-release.
Need help setting up payouts? Visit Support or email hello@getcrew.com.au.

7. Security & Compliance

All escrow funds are held in a dedicated trust account, separate from Crew's operating funds, in accordance with Australian financial services requirements.

  • Card payments are processed by Stripe (PCI DSS Level 1 certified).
  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Crew does not store card numbers — tokenisation is handled by Stripe.
  • Our privacy practices comply with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. See our Privacy Policy.

8. Contact & Support

For questions about a specific payment or escrow holding, contact us through the Help Centre or by email. Please have your job reference number ready.

  • Help Centre: help.getcrew.com.au
  • Email: hello@getcrew.com.au
  • Response time: Within 1 business day

For formal disputes that cannot be resolved through our internal process, you may contact the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC) or your state's consumer affairs office.
