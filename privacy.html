1. About This Policy & Who We Are

Crew Australia Pty Ltd (ACN 000 000 000) ("Crew", "we", "us", "our") operates the Crew home services platform at getcrew.com.au. We are bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) (the "Privacy Act") and the 13 Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) contained in that Act.

This Privacy Policy explains how we manage personal information about our users � including customers, contractors and visitors to our website. "Personal information" has the meaning given to it in the Privacy Act: information or an opinion that could reasonably identify you.

By using the Crew platform or website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. If you have any questions about how we handle personal information, please contact our Privacy Officer (see section 15).

?? This policy complies with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs 1�13). Where this policy refers to a specific APP, that reference is noted in the section heading.

2. What Personal Information We Collect

We collect only the personal information reasonably necessary to provide and improve the Crew platform. The categories of information we collect include:

Account and identity information

  • Full name and date of birth
  • Email address and mobile phone number
  • Profile photograph
  • Password (stored in hashed form � we cannot read it)

Contractor-specific verification information

  • Australian Business Number (ABN) or Australian Company Number (ACN)
  • Trade licence numbers, licence categories and copies of licence documents
  • Current public liability insurance certificate of currency
  • Bank account details for NPP/Osko payouts (BSB and account number)
  • Identity verification documents (e.g. driver licence or passport) � collected only for contractor onboarding and stored securely

Payment and transaction information

  • Partial payment card details (last four digits and card type) retained for display purposes � full card numbers are handled exclusively by our payment processor and are never stored on our systems
  • Transaction history, booking amounts and payout records

Location information

  • Your nominated service suburb or postcode, used for contractor-to-customer matching
  • GPS coordinates, if you grant location permission in the app, used for live job matching and contractor arrival tracking

Platform usage and technical information

  • Device type, operating system and browser version
  • IP address and approximate geographic location derived from IP
  • Pages visited, features used, session duration and referring URLs
  • Search queries and booking history within the platform

Communications and job evidence

  • Messages exchanged between customers and contractors on the platform
  • Job notes, before-and-after photographs and other evidence uploaded by contractors
  • Support communications with the Crew team
  • Reviews and ratings submitted by users

We may also collect sensitive information (such as identity document details) where necessary and with your consent, or where required or authorised by law. We do not use sensitive information for direct marketing.

3. How We Collect Personal Information

We collect personal information in the following ways:

Directly from you

  • When you create or update your account or contractor profile
  • When you book, accept or complete a service on the platform
  • When you submit forms, upload documents or photographs
  • When you contact our support team via email, chat or phone
  • When you submit reviews, ratings or dispute evidence

Automatically through technology

  • Via cookies, pixel tags and similar tracking technologies when you visit our website or use the app (see section 7 � Cookies and Analytics)
  • Through server logs that automatically record IP address, browser type, pages accessed and session activity
  • Via GPS and device location services if you have granted permission

From third parties

  • Identity verification providers used during contractor onboarding
  • Payment processors, for the purpose of confirming transaction status
  • Government licensing databases, to verify trade licence currency
  • Social login providers (e.g. Google or Apple), if you choose to sign in using a third-party service � we receive only the information those services permit and that you authorise

Where it is lawful and practicable to do so, we will tell you at the time of collection why we are collecting your personal information and how it will be used.

4. Why We Collect It and How We Use It

We use personal information for the following purposes:

Providing the Crew platform

  • Creating and managing your account
  • GPS-based matching of customers with nearby available contractors
  • Processing bookings, payments and contractor payouts (including RCTI generation)
  • Facilitating in-app messaging between customers and contractors
  • Displaying booking history, job evidence and ratings

Safety, verification and trust

  • Verifying contractor identities, licences and insurance before they offer services
  • Investigating and resolving disputes between customers and contractors
  • Detecting, preventing and responding to fraud, misconduct or misuse of the platform

Communications and support

  • Sending booking confirmations, job updates and payment receipts
  • Sending important account and service announcements
  • Responding to customer support enquiries
  • Sending direct marketing communications (where you have not opted out � see section 8)

Platform improvement and analytics

  • Analysing aggregated usage data to understand how the platform is used
  • Identifying and fixing technical issues
  • Developing and testing new features and services

Legal and regulatory compliance

  • Maintaining records required under Australian taxation law (including GST and RCTI obligations)
  • Responding to lawful requests from courts, law enforcement or regulatory authorities
  • Enforcing our Terms and Conditions

We will not use your personal information for a purpose other than the purpose for which it was collected (or a directly related purpose) unless you have consented to the alternative use, or that use is permitted or required by law.

5. Disclosure to Third Parties

We may disclose your personal information to the following categories of third parties, only to the extent necessary for the purpose of disclosure:

Payment processors

We use third-party payment processors to handle customer payments and contractor payouts via the New Payments Platform (NPP/Osko). These providers receive the minimum personal and financial information needed to process transactions securely.

Identity verification providers

During contractor onboarding, we use accredited third-party providers to verify identity documents and confirm that trade licences are current and valid.

Analytics providers

We use web and app analytics tools to understand usage patterns. These providers may receive usage data including IP address, device information and session activity. Where possible, we configure analytics tools to anonymise IP addresses before transmission.

Email and notification providers

We use third-party transactional email services to deliver booking confirmations, receipts, alerts and other platform communications to users.

Cloud infrastructure providers

Our platform runs on third-party cloud infrastructure. User data (including personal information) is stored on servers operated by or on behalf of these providers. We select providers that offer strong security standards and data processing agreements.

Professional advisers

We may share personal information with our lawyers, accountants, insurers or other professional advisers where reasonably necessary in the course of seeking advice or conducting our business.

Law enforcement and government authorities

We will disclose personal information to government agencies, law enforcement or regulatory bodies where we are required or authorised to do so by law, or where we reasonably believe disclosure is necessary to prevent imminent harm.

?? We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not share it for the purpose of serving third-party advertising.

6. Overseas Disclosure of Personal Information (APP 8)

?? Australian Privacy Principle 8 � Cross-border disclosure: Before disclosing personal information to an overseas recipient, we take reasonable steps to ensure that the recipient does not breach the APPs in relation to that information.

Some of the third-party service providers we engage are based or operated outside Australia. As a result, certain personal information may be transferred to, stored in or processed in countries other than Australia.

Countries to which your personal information may be disclosed include:

United States of America

  • Database and backend infrastructure: Our backend database and authentication services are provided by Supabase, Inc., a US-headquartered company. Data may be stored on servers located in the United States.
  • Transactional email: Booking confirmations, receipts and platform notifications are delivered via Resend, Inc., a US-based email delivery service.
  • Payment processing: Payment gateway services used for customer payments may be operated by US-headquartered providers.
  • Web analytics: Analytics services we use may process usage data on servers located in the United States.

European Union / European Economic Area

  • Some cloud infrastructure providers may route or store data through servers located in the EU or EEA. Where this occurs, data is subject to the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which provides a level of protection broadly comparable to the APPs.

Where we disclose personal information to overseas recipients, we take reasonable contractual and technical steps to ensure those recipients handle your information consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles. However, by using the Crew platform, you acknowledge that:

  • Some overseas recipients may not be subject to a privacy law equivalent to the APPs
  • The Australian Privacy Commissioner may have limited jurisdiction to investigate complaints about the conduct of overseas entities
  • You may not be able to seek redress under the Privacy Act in respect of how an overseas entity handles your information

If you do not wish your personal information to be disclosed overseas, please contact us at privacy@getcrew.com.au before using the platform. Note that some overseas disclosures are necessary for the platform to function.

7. Cookies and Analytics

Our website and platform use cookies and similar tracking technologies (such as pixel tags and local storage) to improve your experience and measure how our platform is used.

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device when you visit a website. Cookies do not give us access to your device or other information on it beyond what you choose to share.

Types of cookies we use

  • Essential cookies: Required for the platform to operate correctly, including maintaining your authenticated session. These cannot be disabled without disrupting platform functionality.
  • Analytics cookies: Used to collect aggregated information about how visitors use our website, such as pages visited and session duration. This data helps us improve the platform.
  • Preference cookies: Used to remember your settings and preferences, such as your selected role or notification preferences.

You can disable or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling essential cookies will affect your ability to use core features of the platform, including signing in.

Our analytics providers may receive and process usage data including IP address, browser type and pages visited. We configure these tools, where possible, to anonymise IP addresses before transmission overseas.

8. Direct Marketing

We may use your name and contact details to send you information about Crew services, features, promotions or updates that we believe may be of interest to you. We will only send marketing communications in accordance with the Spam Act 2003 (Cth) and the Do Not Call Register Act 2006 (Cth).

You can opt out of marketing communications at any time by:

  • Clicking "Unsubscribe" in the footer of any marketing email we send you
  • Updating your notification preferences in your account settings within the Crew app
  • Contacting us at privacy@getcrew.com.au

We will honour opt-out requests promptly, typically within 5 business days. Opting out of marketing will not affect transactional communications such as booking confirmations or receipts, which are necessary for the operation of your account.

We will never use sensitive information (such as identity documents or government identifiers) for direct marketing purposes.

9. Anonymity and Pseudonymity

In accordance with APP 2, we give individuals the option of not identifying themselves, or using a pseudonym, when dealing with us wherever it is lawful and practicable to do so.

You may browse the Crew website and read general information without creating an account or providing personal information.

However, you must create a verified account to book or offer services on the platform. Verification requires us to collect and hold certain personal information to:

  • Confirm your identity and age eligibility (18 years or older)
  • Verify contractor licences and insurance for licenced trades
  • Comply with our anti-fraud, anti-money-laundering and Know Your Customer (KYC) obligations
  • Enable payment processing and payout services

In these circumstances, it is not practicable for us to deal with you on an anonymous or pseudonymous basis.

10. Security of Personal Information

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Our security measures include:

  • Encryption of all data in transit using TLS/HTTPS
  • Encryption of sensitive data (including identity documents and payment details) at rest
  • Role-based access controls limiting which staff can access personal information
  • Secure, access-controlled storage of identity and financial documents
  • Regular security testing and review of our platform and infrastructure
  • Staff training on data handling and privacy obligations

Despite these measures, no data transmission or storage system is completely secure. If you believe your Crew account has been compromised or you suspect unauthorised access to your personal information, please contact us immediately at security@getcrew.com.au.

We are required to notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) if a data breach is likely to result in serious harm, in accordance with the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme under the Privacy Act.

When personal information is no longer needed for the purpose for which it was collected, and we are not required by law to retain it, we will take reasonable steps to destroy or permanently de-identify that information.

11. Accessing Your Personal Information (APP 12)

Under APP 12 of the Privacy Act, you have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you.

You can update most account information directly within your Crew profile settings at any time. For a broader access request, please contact our Privacy Officer:

  • Email: privacy@getcrew.com.au
  • Subject line: "Personal Information Access Request"
  • Please include your full name, registered email address and a description of the information you wish to access

We will respond to access requests within 30 days. In some cases, we may need to verify your identity before providing access. We will handle access requests free of charge unless the request is excessively broad or requires substantial effort to compile, in which case we will advise you of any fee before proceeding.

We may decline an access request in limited circumstances permitted by the Privacy Act � for example, where providing access would unreasonably impact the privacy of another individual, or where the request relates to information that is the subject of legal proceedings. Where we decline a request (in whole or in part), we will provide you with written reasons.

12. Correcting Your Personal Information (APP 13)

Under APP 13 of the Privacy Act, you have the right to request that we correct personal information we hold about you that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading.

You can correct most account details (such as your name, email address or service area) directly through your account settings. For corrections to information we hold that is not editable in your profile � including contractor licence records or dispute evidence � please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@getcrew.com.au
  • Subject line: "Personal Information Correction Request"
  • Please describe the information you believe is incorrect and what the correct information is

We will take reasonable steps to correct your personal information. If we decide not to make the requested correction, we will notify you in writing with our reasons, and you may request that we attach a statement of your contention to the record.

13. Privacy Complaints & the OAIC

If you believe Crew has breached the Australian Privacy Principles or the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) in relation to your personal information, you have the right to make a complaint.

Step 1 � Complain to Crew first

Please contact our Privacy Officer in the first instance. We ask that you give us an opportunity to address your concern before escalating to the OAIC.

  • Email: privacy@getcrew.com.au
  • Subject line: "Privacy Complaint"
  • Please include your name, contact details and a clear description of your concern

We will acknowledge your complaint within 5 business days and aim to resolve it within 30 days. If we need more time due to complexity, we will let you know.

Step 2 � Refer to the OAIC if unresolved

If you are not satisfied with our response, or if your complaint has not been resolved after 30 days, you may refer the matter to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC):

  • Website: www.oaic.gov.au
  • Phone: 1300 363 992
  • Post: GPO Box 5218, Sydney NSW 2001
  • Online complaint form: www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-complaints
?? The OAIC is the independent statutory authority established under the Privacy Act to handle privacy complaints about private sector organisations. There is no charge for lodging a complaint with the OAIC.
